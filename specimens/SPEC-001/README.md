# SPEC-001 — Tamil Siledai Riddle

**Reasoning Failure & Unsupported Generated Claims**

A frontier AI model was given a classical 15th-century Tamil Siledai (double-meaning riddle) by poet Kalamega Pulavar. Two distinct findings emerged from a single evaluation session:

- **Finding A — Reasoning Failure:** The model proposed and self-rejected two incorrect guesses, stated a third incorrect answer, then explicitly requested the correct answer from the evaluator before it could explain the riddle's phonetic-fusion solution (பாம்பு + எள் = பாம்பெள்).
- **Finding B — Unsupported Generated Claims:** Immediately afterward, while drafting unrelated promotional content about the exchange, the model generated two specific, unsupported factual claims — an incorrect historical date and a fabricated account of testing against three separate AI models — neither of which is supported by any evidence in this record.

**Severity:** Finding A — Low operational impact, high research value. Finding B — Moderate.
