Project CHRONOS

CHRONOS is an independent frontier AI auditing methodology focused on identifying, documenting, and analyzing real-world failures in advanced AI systems using reproducible evidence.

Research Focus

  • Hallucination Detection
  • Reasoning Failures
  • Long-context Degradation
  • Temporal Logic Errors
  • Tamil Linguistic Evaluation
  • Evidence-based AI Auditing
