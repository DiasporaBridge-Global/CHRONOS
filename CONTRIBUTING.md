# Contributing to Project CHRONOS

Project CHRONOS is maintained by Sirajudeen Seethapathy.

## Ownership and permission

- SPEC-001 through SPEC-004 are existing, owner-maintained specimens. Their historical versions previously distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 remain subject to that licence. This policy does not retrospectively revoke or narrow those grants.
- New original CHRONOS-authored material first published after the prospective rights policy takes effect is © 2026 Sirajudeen Seethapathy. All rights reserved, unless a separate written licence states otherwise.
- Submission, discussion, or a pull request does not by itself transfer copyright or grant CHRONOS permission to publish or reuse contributed material.
- Before an external contribution is accepted, the contributor must document authority to submit it and sign a written agreement granting the specific rights CHRONOS requires, or execute a valid assignment where agreed. No rights transfer is presumed.
- CHRONOS may decline a proposed contribution where ownership, provenance, permissions, privacy, or third-party rights cannot be established.
- Contributors must not submit confidential, personal, copyrighted, or third-party material unless they have a lawful basis and the necessary permissions.

## Submission standards

All proposed specimens and methodology changes must follow [the Specimen Structure Standard](methodology/specimen-structure-standard.md), preserve source provenance, and distinguish raw evidence from extracted facts and analysis.

## Review

Submitting a contribution does not guarantee acceptance. The Project Owner determines whether a contribution meets the project's evidence, quality, rights, and security requirements.

## Third-party material

Third-party screenshots, documents, quotations, datasets, software, logos, and other content remain subject to their respective rights holders' terms and applicable law. Contributors must identify such material and its source; inclusion in CHRONOS does not make it CHRONOS-owned.
