# Contributing to Project CHRONOS

Project CHRONOS is maintained by Sirajudeen Seethapathy.

## Closed specimen archive

The GitHub specimen archive is closed and final. It contains exactly four specimens: SPEC-001, SPEC-002, SPEC-003, and SPEC-004.

**No new specimens will be accepted, submitted, created, or added to this GitHub repository.** Pull requests or contributions proposing additional specimens must be declined. The specimen identifiers and archive scope are fixed at SPEC-001 through SPEC-004.

This closure does not prevent corrections, maintenance, or documentation improvements to the four existing specimens and the repository's methodology, provided they do not introduce another specimen.

## Ownership and permission

- SPEC-001 through SPEC-004 are existing, owner-maintained specimens. Their historical versions previously distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 remain subject to that licence. This policy does not retrospectively revoke or narrow those grants.
- Sirajudeen Seethapathy is the owner of original CHRONOS-authored work, subject to applicable law and valid third-party or contributor rights.
- Submission, discussion, or a pull request does not by itself transfer copyright or grant CHRONOS permission to publish or reuse contributed material.
- CHRONOS may decline proposed changes where ownership, provenance, permissions, privacy, or third-party rights cannot be established.

## Permitted repository contributions

Only maintenance and corrections to the four existing specimens, methodology, documentation, and repository infrastructure may be considered. Such changes must preserve source provenance and distinguish raw evidence from extracted facts and analysis. They must not create, register, or add a fifth or later specimen.

## Review

Submitting a contribution does not guarantee acceptance. The Project Owner determines whether a proposed maintenance change meets the project's evidence, quality, rights, and security requirements.

## Third-party material

Third-party screenshots, documents, quotations, datasets, software, logos, and other content remain subject to their respective rights holders' terms and applicable law. Contributors must identify such material and its source; inclusion in CHRONOS does not make it CHRONOS-owned.
